# Example: Claude 3-Part narrative page

This is a planning example, not a claim about a specific current model version.

## Page beat

Claude feels less like a chat window and more like a collaborator that can hold onto a longer task.

## Exact copy

1. Claude 又更新了，我最明显的感觉不是“更聪明”。
2. 而是很多以前要反复解释的事，它开始能自己接住。
3. 长任务做下来，越来越像一个能一起干活的搭档。

## Direction

- **Mode:** 3-Part
- **Style:** Black Ink + Soft Accent Color
- **Scene A:** The canonical character opens a laptop and notices an update.
- **Scene B:** The character passes a tall stack of documents to a small assistant figure.
- **Scene C:** Both sit side by side, working at one desk.
- **Layout:** Three unequal, interleaved beats; one large sentence, two medium text blocks, no panels or numbered headings in the final image.
- **Accent palette:** Pale blue laptop screen and pale yellow document tabs; combined colored area under 15%.
- **Output:** Illustration without body copy, followed by exact Chinese typesetting in SVG or HTML at 1242 × 1660.
